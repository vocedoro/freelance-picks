About

Freelance Picks is run by a small independent team. We write practical guides that help people find reliable freelancers on Fiverr for everyday projects.

Each guide explains what to prepare, which options exist, and how to judge a seller's profile: review count, rating, activity and portfolio. We link to Fiverr search pages so you can compare sellers yourself.

We earn a commission if you buy through some of our links, which keeps the site free. It does not change the price you pay. See the disclosure.

We are not Fiverr and we do not represent any seller. We do not guarantee any outcome from a purchase.
